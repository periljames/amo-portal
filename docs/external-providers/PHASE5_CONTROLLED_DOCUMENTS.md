# Phase 5 — controlled provider documents, agreements and DMS/QMS integration

**Implementation branch:** `codex/external-provider-platform-20261009`. **Only PR:** #560. **Status:** source implementation committed; migration and runtime acceptance deliberately deferred to Phase 10. Phase 6 has not started.

## Canonical authority and scope

- `ProcurementSupplier` remains the tenant-specific provider master; Procurement/Finance maintain transactions and commercial records. Contract records are the existing `quality_external_provider_contracts`, not an external-provider parallel contract repository.
- DMS `manuals`, `manual_revisions` and `document_retention_dispositions` remain the authoritative published controlled-document, exact-revision, legal-hold and disposition systems. Only DMS users can publish, approve, control retention or dispose; there is no provider-side DMS token or blanket Quality document administration.
- `quality_external_provider_evidence` remains QMS's authoritative verification register. Accepting an uploaded **submission** only creates a **PENDING** QMS record. Independent QMS decision, qualification gates and provider work authorization remain separate.
- Existing external identity, cookie/CSRF, tenant/provider membership, per-member document/contract grants and authorization checks continue. Prospective suppliers may submit qualification documents under explicitly delegated credentials, without permission to execute work.

## Implementation

1. The `extprov_261015_docs` migration adds category-specific tenant policy, controlled form revision, optional expiry/lead-days and retention-reference fields. No category or universal retention period is imposed by migration. The Quality administration screen offers all requested record-class presets as opt-in examples, including agreements and amendments, statutory/approval/insurance/capability records, QMS manuals and questionnaires, audit/CAPA/training/calibration/test/component-release/conformity/repair/work-order/technical/Authority documents and forms/acknowledgements.
2. `external_provider_documents_router.py` provides protected partner requirement lists, authorized exact published form download, classified document intake, scoped submission history and original downloads. Files are validated by filename, declared MIME, magic/signature, DOCX package contents, bounded 20-MB streaming hash, and mandatory configured ClamAV scanning. Originals are stored unchanged in the existing storage adapter; staging is not publication. The client has progress, cancellation and stable retry keys. The upload does not call DMS publication.
3. The internal Quality/Document Control queue supports download and `ACCEPTED`, `RETURNED`, `REJECTED` with explanation. Accepted evidence creates a **PENDING** canonical QMS evidence reference; QMS must separately VERIFY. A DMS link is accepted only when the current published source revision's SHA-256 matches the staged original exactly, and existing DMS restricted-manual visibility is respected. Immutable append-only events and original source, actor, size/hash and review provenance are recorded.
4. Previous originals are not deleted. Resubmission points to the earlier submission under a **tenant + provider composite FK**; the old accepted evidence is superseded only when its valid replacement has completed independent QMS verification, with immutable history. Stored `SUPERSEDED` state and old original remain discoverable after suspension/closure.
5. Agreements are edited on existing QMS contract records, with counterparties, approved sites, authority reference, terms, notice period, current published DMS revision and accepted matching signed evidence. Non-draft contract metadata cannot be overwritten. Contract activation and Procurement's mandatory-contract use gate require the active, date-valid agreement, current published DMS original and accepted signed source independently verified by QMS; Procurement overrides do not waive a mandatory agreement. Such a contract **never creates supplier Quality approval**.
6. Existing scheduled Quality worker posts deduplicated Quality Manager reminders for evidence expiry using **tenant-configured days** and for active agreement notice windows from their recorded notice periods. It does not introduce a duplicate scheduler, audit engine or CAR process.
7. Internal document queue projects matching canonical DMS revision-retention status, legal hold and retention-until date. Legal hold/disposition decisions remain inside DMS. An absent retention registration is explicitly displayed as such, not silently assumed.
8. Legacy unclassified upload/decision endpoints no longer accept new files or independent decisions; their existing read-only history remains visible.

## Administrative workflow

Document Control first publishes the latest authorized form/revision in DMS and grants only the intended provider document access. Quality Manager configures a tenant/provider-category requirement. The provider downloads the allowed form if linked, uploads a classified document under its own scope, and receives a server-issued submission reference. Quality or its permitted Document Control delegate assesses the original and retains reasons. If accepted, QMS creates a pending independent evidence review; Quality Manager verifies or rejects through the existing canonical QMS endpoint. DMS governs any published source, distribution, retention/legal hold and disposition. Contract owner ties an executed agreement to its matching signed source; Quality may activate only after the separate QMS verified-evidence gate.

## Deployment dependencies and remaining Phase 10 acceptance

- Configure **`PROVIDER_DOCUMENT_CLAMD_HOST`** (and optional `PROVIDER_DOCUMENT_CLAMD_PORT`, default 3310) to a reachable malware scanner before enabling intake. The upload endpoint deliberately rejects with 503 if scanning is unavailable. This is not evidence that production antivirus/Content Disarm & Reconstruction has been provisioned.
- External uploads are bounded at 20 MB, single-request streaming; progress, cancellation and idempotent retries are included. **True interrupted-upload byte-range resume or multipart transfers for larger technical data are not implemented** and must be assessed against existing storage before production acceptance. No destructive PDF/Word conversion occurs.
- `retention_policy_ref` is tenant metadata, **not a verified DMS disposition policy binding**. A QMS-only staging object without a DMS-controlled revision does not yet receive a DMS legal-hold/disposition record. This remains an important governance integration gap, not an assertion of Phase 5 regulatory/production completion. DMS-backed revisions show their actual disposition record and legal hold; never assume absence means released for deletion.
- Signed evidence is subjected to human Quality verification and exact original matching; this does not cryptographically validate a handwritten/digital signature or substitute for authority acceptance. Authority evidence/applicability remains a separate tenant decision.
- None of the modified sources, schemas or screens have been migrated, built, linted, typechecked, exercised in a browser, load-tested or run through tests/CI by this implementation. Automatic GitHub CI triggered by commits is not an accepted green result without inspection. See `PHASE10_VERIFICATION_LEDGER.md` for exact verification obligations. Do not merge PR #560 or claim production readiness until Phase 10.
