# EASA Reliability Profile

**Official source verified:** 2026-08-07  
**Current source used:** EASA Easy Access Rules for Continuing Airworthiness, September 2025 revision: https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/easy-access-rules-continuing-airworthiness

## Regulatory basis

The baseline profile separates binding continuing-airworthiness/maintenance-programme requirements from AMC/GM guidance. It focuses on Regulation (EU) No 1321/2014, M.A.302 and Appendix I to AMC M.A.302 where a Reliability Programme applies.

## Controlled applicability

Reliability Programme applicability is not assumed merely because the module exists. The tenant must retain evidence of the approved maintenance-programme basis and applicability, including MSG-3/condition-monitoring circumstances where relevant.

The mandatory baseline gate `EASA-MA302-APPLICABILITY-AND-AMP-CONTROL` remains `GAP` until current tenant applicability and maintenance-programme control are evidenced.

## Analytical/reporting mapping

The profile supports the Appendix I concepts for:

- identifying the controlled aircraft/system/component population;
- relevant pilot, technical, maintenance, shop, stores, operational and special-operation evidence;
- data display capable of exposing trends and adverse performance;
- performance standards/alert levels where applicable;
- repetitive-defect and deterioration analysis;
- maintenance findings and modification/service-information review;
- corrective action and effectiveness assessment;
- defined organisational responsibility;
- routine reporting and distribution;
- continuous programme effectiveness review;
- maintenance-programme change recommendations and the applicable approval route.

## Maintenance-programme protection

A Reliability result or AMP recommendation never updates a task/interval directly. The controlled recommendation lifecycle retains technical basis, review, authority-approval requirement, implementation and effectiveness monitoring. The actual AMP remains governed by its own approval/control process.
