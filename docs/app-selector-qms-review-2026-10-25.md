# App selector and QMS navigation review

The tenant shell now exposes Apps in the header, drawer and profile. Its full-screen selector presents permission-filtered operational workspaces, supports mouse/touch and arrow-key browsing, and requires an explicit Open app action. Closing it preserves the mounted page and unsaved form state. The current app resumes the existing page. The assigned login landing page is unchanged. Administration is offered when Admin View is active; standing administrators retain their operational app access. Explicitly removed module grants cannot be recreated by department metadata.

The aviation background uses local SVG flight paths and CSS cloud/aircraft animation, without a video stream or third-party service. It pauses when hidden or when the user chooses Pause background. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Focus stays in the selector and returns to the app-switch button when it closes. Content uses an isolated dark palette so light-theme rules cannot obscure headings. The close control remains visible while scrolling.

Provider navigation review found that evaluations, audits and findings previously all rendered the provider register. Those destinations now use their distinct existing bounded registers. Approved and expired provider views request their respective governed provider statuses, show distinct headings and retain type/risk/search filters. Provider records, qualifications, contracts and vendor authentication keep their existing owners and permissions.

Verification distinguishes isolated role fixtures from live authenticated checks. No real vendor account is impersonated, invitation sent, approval granted or business record changed for UI testing.

The initial live review was interrupted by the VM outage recorded in `vm-boot-incident-2026-10-25.md`. After service recovery, the new production selector was visually verified with the supplied authenticated administrator at 1440 by 900 pixels. Its aircraft animation was running, operational apps were available, and Administration remained gated by Admin View. Opening Training & Competence from QMS reached its actual Control Room; switching back to QMS succeeded. Seven selector browser scenarios and 53 app/navigation unit checks passed. Normal-user and provider-route checks use isolated fixtures; no live vendor account or vendor approval workflow was exercised.
